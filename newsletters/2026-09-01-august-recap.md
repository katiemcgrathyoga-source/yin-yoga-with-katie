Hi {$name|default:"there"},

Most of you joined this list in the last few weeks, so this is the first of these

There's nothing to keep up with.

**A series where I stop talking**

I've been quietly making something a little different. It's called
[Loving Silence](https://yinyogawithkatie.com/loving-silence) — five classes where I
settle you into each shape, give you what you need to know, and then say nothing at
all until it's time to move.

One of my core philosophies has always been minimal cueing, and this takes it as far
as it goes. Some people find it a relief straight away. Others find the first minute
strange and then wouldn't go back. There's a full body class, one for hips, one for
the lower back, one for the evenings you can't seem to put the day down, and one for
bed.

They're on the channel for members from today. [Have a read of what they are and
who they suit](https://yinyogawithkatie.com/loving-silence) — and if you'd rather be
talked through your practice, nothing has changed, everything else still is.

**And something free, for the outside of your knee**

New on the site this month:
[The Outside Line](https://yinyogawithkatie.com/routines/the-outside-line) — a gentle
routine for the outer hip, the front of the thigh and the long side line running down
toward the knee. It's the one I'd give any runner who has been told to stretch their
IT band and found it didn't do much.

The nice part is you don't have to watch a clock. The site holds the timer for you,
tells you when to swap sides, and rings a small bell when it's time to come out, so
you can close your eyes and stay where you are. No props, and you're welcome to make
every hold shorter.

I also wrote down [why you can't actually stretch an IT
band](https://yinyogawithkatie.com/blog/can-you-stretch-your-it-band), and what to do
instead.

**Also in August**

[Yin Yoga for Burnout Recovery](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1-_ftSLJE-Q) — a full
hour, for when tired has gone somewhere past tired.

Take good care of yourself.

Katie
