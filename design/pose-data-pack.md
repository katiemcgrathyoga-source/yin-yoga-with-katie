## Ankle Stretch

- slug: `ankle-stretch`
- target areas: ankles, shins, feet, knees
- level: intermediate
- hold: 1–3 minutes
- props: blanket, bolster
- photo: YES — /poses/ankle-stretch.jpg
- benefits:
  - Stretches the fronts of the ankles and the tops of the feet, an area that stays short in most of us.
  - Lengthens along the shins and stimulates the four meridian lines that run through the feet and ankles — stomach, spleen, liver and gall bladder.
  - The natural counter to Toe Squat, balancing the front and back of the ankle.
  - Opens and gently strengthens the ankle joints, a welcome release after squatting, kneeling or seated meditation.

## Banana

- slug: `banana`
- target areas: side body, IT band, obliques, outer hips, ribs
- level: beginner
- hold: 3–5 minutes per side
- props: cushion
- photo: YES — /poses/banana.jpg
- benefits:
  - A long, luxurious stretch down the whole side of the body, from the fingertips to the ankle — a simple lateral flexion (side bend) of the spine.
  - Reaches the IT band and outer hip, the side ribs and the oblique muscles, stretching the gall bladder line that runs along the side seam.
  - Creates space between the ribs and the hip, so the breath can expand into the side of the lungs; reaching the arms overhead adds a stretch through the heart and lung lines of the inner arm.
  - Simple and deeply soothing — a lovely, quiet way to begin or end a practice.

## Butterfly

- slug: `butterfly`
- target areas: inner thighs, hips, lower back, spine
- level: beginner
- hold: 3–5 minutes
- props: bolster, cushion
- photo: YES — /poses/butterfly.jpg
- benefits:
  - Opens the inner thighs, groin and hips while the fold lengthens the whole back line — a lovely two-for-one shape.
