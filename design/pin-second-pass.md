Re-measured limits — four sizes moved, so please re-check the copy against these
Measured on the 872px text column (64px margins) at the average advance of each face — 0.44em for Cormorant Garamond 500, 0.50em for Cabin 400.

Headline ≤40 on the benefit card — 125px gives 15 characters a line, so 40 characters is 3 lines and, worst case, 4. Ceiling is 4 lines at 510px. One new rule: no single word longer than 15 characters. Current copy's longest is “sitting.” at 8.
