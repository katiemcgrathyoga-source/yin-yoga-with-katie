# Runner SEO Content Cluster — Journal briefs

A topic cluster to feed the funnel (free post-run class → **The Runner's Reset**).
Strategy: don't fight big publishers for the "yoga for runners" head term — own the
specific, high-intent long-tail runners actually search, and link each spoke up to
the pillar and down to a pose page + the free class.

**Rules for every post**
- Claims discipline: improves range of motion, restores mobility, supports recovery
  & relaxation, eases the tightness running builds up. **Never** "flushes toxins/
  lactic acid", "lengthens the IT band", "prevents/cures/fixes injury". Pair any
  health statement with a gentle "not medical advice — see a physio for pain" line.
- Katie's voice: warm, unhurried, plain, options not commands ("you can", "you
  might"), the edge is "about 60–80% of your capacity", sign-off "take good care of
  yourself". No emoji, no exclamation spam.
- Same closing CTA block: free class → course. 2–3 internal links (up to the pillar,
  out to pose pages). Repurpose-once: each post → one Pinterest pin + one short.
- Cadence: ~1/week. Pillar first, then spokes 1 → 3 → 6 (converters + the shareable
  myth-buster) early.
