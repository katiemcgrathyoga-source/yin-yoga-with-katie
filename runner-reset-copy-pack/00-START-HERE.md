# The Runner's Reset — Copywriting Pack

Everything Claude needs to write the launch copy (sales page, lead-magnet funnel,
email nurture + founding-launch sequence) for **The Runner's Reset**.

## How to use it
1. Open a **new Claude conversation**.
2. **Attach the files in this folder** (drag them all in).
3. **Paste the contents of `01-PROMPT.md`** as your message.
4. Claude will first propose the avatar, big idea, promise, and guarantee, then
   pause for your okay before writing the full copy.
