Ten minutes, no props, and really just one shape done twice.

You sit in **[Shoelace](/poses/shoelace)** — half if the full version is too much — and settle there. Then **[Eagle](/poses/eagle) arms** are layered on top of it: one arm out, the other threading underneath, holding opposite shoulders or twisting the forearms so the palms meet. Elbows lift only as far as they lift.

So the hips and the outer hip are working, and the shoulders and upper back are working, at the same time, for two minutes.

Then the arms come down and a **[seated twist](/poses/seated-twist)** takes the spine — hand behind you, other arm hooked against the top knee, inhale to lengthen and exhale to turn.

The whole round again on the other side, some windshield wipers, and that's it.

It's the stacking that makes this fit into ten minutes. Most short classes rush through six poses; this one holds three areas at once and doesn't rush any of them.
