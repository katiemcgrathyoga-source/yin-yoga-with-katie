Class 3 of **Loving Silence**. Most lower-back classes ask you to fold forward, which is the shape you have already been in all day.

So this one goes the other ways instead. Sphinx is a soft backbend, Reclined Twist is rotation, Banana is the side bend almost nothing else reaches, and Knees to Chest is the single gentle rounding at the end. Crocodile sits in the middle as a rebound, which is where a lot of the relief actually happens.

This is general care, not treatment. If a doctor has named something in your back, check with your physiotherapist before you follow along.
