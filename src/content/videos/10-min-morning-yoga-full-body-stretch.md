A morning sequence rather than a Yin class — and Katie says as much in the first ten seconds. This is Hatha, with each shape held about a minute, because a stiff early-morning body doesn't want five-minute passive holds.

**[Child's Pose](/poses/childs-pose)** to arrive, hands forward if you want the shoulders in it.

Then **tabletop**, a few rounds of **Cat-Cow** with the breath, and swaying the hips side to side to look over each shoulder — which loosens the side body more than it sounds like it should.

**Downward Dog**, heels reaching for the mat, knees soft, head down between the arms.

Then the main piece, each side: a **low lunge** with the back knee down, coming up into **Crescent** with the arms overhead, then **cactus arms** drawing the elbows back to open the chest, or fingers interlaced behind the back for more. Back through Downward Dog and repeat.

A few quiet breaths sitting, and that's ten minutes.

If you want long, still Yin holds, this isn't that one — but as something to do before the day starts, it does the job.
