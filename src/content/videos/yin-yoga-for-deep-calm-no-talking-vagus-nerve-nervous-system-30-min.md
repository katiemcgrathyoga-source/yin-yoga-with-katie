Class 4 of **Loving Silence**. For the evenings you get all the way to the end of the day and still can't seem to put it down.

Everything here is supported and nearly everything is on your back. Supported Fish opens the front of the body after a day of closing it; Reclined Twist takes the spine; Child's Pose is somewhere small to fold into; Legs Up the Wall does more for heavy legs and a busy head than its effort level suggests.

Long, still, quiet holds are one of the more dependable ways to help the body settle, and taking the voice out of the room seems to help. Nothing here is forced, and you can come out whenever you like.
