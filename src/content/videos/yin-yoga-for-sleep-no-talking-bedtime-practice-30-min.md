Class 5 of **Loving Silence**, and the one to reach for last thing at night.

Everything is supported and nearly everything is lying down: Reclined Butterfly over a bolster, Reclined Twist through the spine, Caterpillar down the back line, and Legs Up the Wall for legs that have carried you all day. Corpse at the end, for as long as you like.

Long, passive holds give the body a slow signal that the day is over — which is rather more useful at bedtime than anything effortful. Dim the lights, stay in your pyjamas, and if you fall asleep before the end, that is the practice working.
