This is the Yin one. Five shapes, held for a few quiet minutes each, working the chain running loads most — inner thigh, the front of the hip, the glute and outer hip, then the whole back line.

Long, still holds are what reach the deeper tissue around the hips and joints, and a rest day is exactly when to give them the time they need.
