A bedtime class that does the hips first and the shoulders last, which is the right order if you're heading straight to bed afterwards.

The round is three shapes, two minutes each. **[Sleeping Swan](/poses/sleeping-swan)** for the glute and outer hip, front foot flexed to look after the knee, weight kept even rather than collapsed into the front hip. **[Shoelace](/poses/shoelace)** — full or half — with a side-body reach added for the last few breaths, one hand walking out and the other arcing over. Then **[Half Butterfly](/poses/half-butterfly)**, one leg long, folding over it with a pillow under the bent knee if it floats.

All three again on the other side.

Then **[Thread the Needle](/poses/thread-the-needle)** for two minutes each way, which is where the upper back and shoulders let go, and a few rounds of Cat-Cow with the hips swaying side to side to finish.

The line worth taking to bed is the one Katie offers during Shoelace: the day is over, and all your thinking is done for today.
