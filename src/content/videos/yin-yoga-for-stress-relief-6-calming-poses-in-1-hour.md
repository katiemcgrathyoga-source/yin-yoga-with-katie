This is the one for an evening when you want a proper deep stretch and have no intention of working for it. Katie says as much at the start: it's for the times you don't want to put much effort into your practice.

**Reclined [Butterfly](/poses/butterfly)** for five minutes, head on the bolster, feet as far from the pelvis as feels right.

**[Saddle](/poses/saddle)** for five — feet either side of the hips rather than under them, leaning back onto the bolster if you're invited to, half saddle if not.

**[Half Butterfly](/poses/half-butterfly)** for five minutes each side with your forehead resting on blocks, which is what makes five minutes possible.

**[Supported Bridge](/poses/supported-bridge)** for five, blocks under the tailbone, walking the feet away for more.

**[Knees hugged in](/poses/wind-relieving)** for five, becoming **[Happy Baby](/poses/happy-baby)** for the last two if you like.

**[Supported Fish](/poses/supported-fish)** for three, a block behind the shoulder blades and another under the head if the crown reaching the floor is too much.

Then the strap comes out: a two-minute hamstring hold with the leg straight up, then the leg carried across into **[Cat Pulling its Tail](/poses/cat-pulling-its-tail)** for three. Both sides.

Five minutes in **[Corpse](/poses/corpse)**.

The line she keeps returning to, and it's a good one: no catastrophe will unfold if you don't think about that for the next hour.
