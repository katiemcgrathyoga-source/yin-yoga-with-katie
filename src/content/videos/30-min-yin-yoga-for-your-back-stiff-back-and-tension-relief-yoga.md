A back class that takes the spine in every direction it goes, in a sensible order.

**Extension first.** **[Sphinx](/poses/sphinx)** for three minutes, elbows shoulder-width, sliding them further forward if the lower back objects — and cushions under the belly or pelvis are fair game. **[Crocodile](/poses/crocodile)** to rest. Then **[Seal](/poses/seal)** for two minutes if you want the deeper version, and Crocodile again, looking the other way.

**Then rounding.** **[Child's Pose](/poses/childs-pose)** for three minutes with the knees close together, which is what lets the back actually round. Followed by side bends — hands walked over to the left, then the right, six breaths each — for the side of the ribcage.

**Then rotation.** **[Dragon](/poses/dragon)** on each side, then coming up out of the lunge into a prayer twist with the elbow hooked outside the front thigh. It wobbles. Pick a spot on the floor, and falling out of it doesn't matter.

**Then length.** **[Dangling](/poses/dangling)** for two minutes, knees soft, head heavy. Tight hamstrings pull on the pelvis and leave the lower back doing the work, so this belongs in a back class more than it looks like it does.
