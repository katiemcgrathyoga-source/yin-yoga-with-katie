There are only four Yin poses in this thirty-five minutes. The rest is sitting, breathing and resting, which is rather the point.

It opens with six minutes before a single shape: twenty slow breaths, hands at the chest, and Katie's line that comes back through the whole class — **you are not your thoughts, you are the observer of your thoughts**. Then some loose movement to shake the day off.

**[Child's Pose](/poses/childs-pose)** for two minutes. **[Dragon](/poses/dragon)** for a minute a side, each one rolling into **[Twisted Dragon](/poses/twisted-dragon)** — she describes it as wringing out a dish cloth — with a Downward Dog between and after.

**[Sphinx](/poses/sphinx)** for two minutes across the chest, resting in **[Crocodile](/poses/crocodile)**, then **[Seal](/poses/seal)** for one minute if the deeper backbend is welcome. Crocodile again, and while you're there, three things in your life that are going well.

It closes the way it opened: twenty breaths, and a deliberate smile that nobody can see.

If you only have six minutes tonight, do the beginning.
