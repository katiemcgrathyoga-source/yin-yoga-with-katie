A short bedtime practice with very little in it, which is the point at that hour.

It opens sitting still with **4:8 breathing** — in for four, out for eight. The long exhale is what does the work, and a few minutes of it changes the tone of everything that follows.

Then **[Swan](/poses/swan)** for three minutes a side, with **[Sleeping Swan](/poses/sleeping-swan)** offered as the deeper version if you want to fold forward and rest your head on your hands. This is the big one for the glutes, the outer hip and the groin, and a **Downward Dog** between the sides shakes it out.

**[Child's Pose](/poses/childs-pose)** for a minute, and then everything happens lying down: a **[reclined twist](/poses/reclined-twist)** held three full minutes each way, shoulders staying on the floor, and three quiet minutes in **[Corpse](/poses/corpse)** to close.

No props, nothing to fetch, nothing that needs you to be flexible. If your forehead doesn't reach the mat in Child's Pose, stack your hands under it.
