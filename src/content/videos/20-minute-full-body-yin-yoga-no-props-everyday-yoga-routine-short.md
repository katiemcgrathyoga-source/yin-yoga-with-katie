This one starts at the floor and works up.

**[Toe Squat](/poses/toe-squat)** opens it — six breaths on the soles of the feet, which is a lot of sensation for very little time, and the pose most people skip. Slide the little toe out from under, keep the weight on the balls of the feet, and come out of it if the knees or ankles object.

**[Child's Pose](/poses/childs-pose)** settles things for two minutes, then reaches the arms long to bring the shoulders in.

The middle is **[Dragon](/poses/dragon)**, two minutes a side, into the front of the hip. For the last stretch of each hold there's the option of **[Twisted Dragon](/poses/twisted-dragon)** — same shape, rotation added — and a **Downward Dog** between the sides to shake it out.

Then upwards: **[Eagle](/poses/eagle) arms** for the upper back and shoulders, **[Squat](/poses/squat)** for a minute, **[Dangling](/poses/dangling)** for a minute down the back of the legs, and a short **[seated twist](/poses/seated-twist)** each way to finish.

Nothing here needs a prop, which is the point — it travels.
