Class 2 of **Loving Silence**. The hips are where most of us keep the day — sitting, stress, standing badly on one leg for twenty minutes at a bus stop. This one goes at them from every angle and then leaves you alone to feel it.

Butterfly for the inner thighs, Sleeping Swan for the outer hips and glutes, Dragon for the hip flexors, and Reclined Twist to finish through the spine and outer hip. Around three minutes each, with long rests in between.

If a hip pinches rather than spreads, ease off — that is bone meeting bone, and time won't change it. Reclined Swan and a reclined Dragon option are both shown on screen if you'd rather stay off your knees.
