Two poses. Both on your back. Twenty minutes.

It starts before any shape at all, lying in **[Corpse](/poses/corpse)** while Katie walks a wave of relaxation down through you — jaw, shoulders, spine, hips, legs — and says the thing worth hearing at that hour: the day is done, you've done your best, and anything still on your mind can be thought about tomorrow.

**Reclined Cobbler's Pose** — reclined **[Butterfly](/poses/butterfly)** — for four minutes. Soles together, knees simply hanging, feet further away for less and closer for more. Nothing pushed down. Her image for it is gravity as a blanket laid gently across you.

A rest, a moment with the **[knees hugged in](/poses/wind-relieving)**, then the **[reclined twist](/poses/reclined-twist)** for three full minutes each way. Shoulders stay on the floor, knees higher or lower to taste, and if turning your head bothers your neck, don't.

Then **[Corpse](/poses/corpse)**, for as long as you like — or you can simply stop and get into bed, which is arguably the point.
