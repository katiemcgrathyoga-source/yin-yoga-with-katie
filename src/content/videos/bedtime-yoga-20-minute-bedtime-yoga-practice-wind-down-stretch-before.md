A bedtime practice that moves a little first, then settles. Worth saying plainly: this isn't a long-hold Yin class, and the title doesn't pretend otherwise. Most shapes here get three or four breaths rather than three or four minutes.

It opens with **Cat-Cow**, moving with the breath, and a deliberately casual **Downward Dog** — paddle the feet, sway the hips, knees bent, heels wherever they land.

Then the shapes get quieter. **[Thread the Needle](/poses/thread-the-needle)** on each side for the upper back and shoulders. **[Puppy](/poses/puppy)** for three breaths, elbows bent if the arms tingle. **[Child's Pose](/poses/childs-pose)**, which Katie frames as your own cocoon — anything trying to get in can be told you'll think about it another time.

Onto your back for reclined **[Butterfly](/poses/butterfly)**, knees hanging rather than pushed, then a **[reclined twist](/poses/reclined-twist)** each way, **[knees hugged in](/poses/wind-relieving)**, and two minutes in **[Corpse](/poses/corpse)**.

If your body wants to move a bit before it lies down, this is the one. If you want to be still from the first minute, take one of the long-hold bedtime classes instead.
