Class 1 of **Loving Silence**. Katie settles you into each shape, tells you how long you'll be there, and then says nothing at all until it's time to move.

A full-body practice: Supported Fish for the chest, Sleeping Swan for the hips, Reclined Twist through the spine, Dragon into the hip flexors, and Caterpillar down the back line — each held two to three minutes, with long rests in between. The resting is as much the practice as the poses.

Every pose above links to its full guide if you'd like the cues written down before you begin.
