A bedtime class with five shapes in it and nothing to go and find first.

**[Child's Pose](/poses/childs-pose)** for three minutes opens it. Knees wide reaches the hips; knees together reaches the lower back and spine. Both are correct.

**[Melting Heart](/poses/melting-heart)** — anahatasana — for two minutes, hips staying over the knees so the chest sinks rather than the lower back. If your hands tingle, bend the elbows and bring them in.

**[Swan](/poses/swan)** into **[Sleeping Swan](/poses/sleeping-swan)**, three minutes a side, with a Downward Dog between. This is the long one, into the glute and the front of the back leg, and the cue that matters is keeping the hips square to the front of the mat rather than sinking into the front hip.

Then everything is on your back. **Reclined [Butterfly](/poses/butterfly)** for three minutes, soles together, knees left alone. A **[reclined twist](/poses/reclined-twist)** for two minutes each way. And **[Corpse](/poses/corpse)** — stay longer than the video does if you can, because there's nothing after this.
