A fully propped half hour, and one instruction running through all of it: **make the out-breath longer than the in-breath.** In for four, out for six or eight. That's what makes this a nervous-system class rather than a propped stretch.

**[Constructive Rest](/poses/constructive-rest)** to begin, head on the bolster, knees resting together, with slow knee drops side to side.

**Supported [Child's Pose](/poses/childs-pose)** for four minutes with the bolster raised on blocks — or, if Child's Pose isn't available, stay on your back and hug the knees in.

A **[Fish and Butterfly hybrid](/poses/supported-fish-butterfly)** for three minutes: lying back over the bolster so the chest opens, soles of the feet together, knees left where they fall.

**Supported [Caterpillar](/poses/caterpillar)** for three, with the bolster on your legs and blocks under your head — and if your forehead is on a block, roll it gently side to side. It's a small thing that settles something.

A **[supine twist](/poses/reclined-twist)** for two minutes each way, then five minutes with your **[legs up the wall](/poses/legs-up-the-wall)**, where gravity does the whole job.

The sentence worth borrowing, from early on: whatever brought you to the mat, nothing catastrophic will happen if you don't think about it for the next twenty-four minutes. You can schedule the worrying for later.
