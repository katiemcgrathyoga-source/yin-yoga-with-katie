Four poses in forty minutes, and a quarter of the class is lying still at the end.

**Reclined Cobbler's Pose** — reclined **[Butterfly](/poses/butterfly)** — opens it for five minutes, soles together, knees left exactly where they fall. Feet further from the pelvis makes it lighter. Then a minute with the **[knees hugged to the chest](/poses/wind-relieving)** to let it settle.

**[Caterpillar](/poses/caterpillar)** for four minutes down the whole back body, feet active rather than flexed or flopped, with a seated hug afterwards as the rebound.

**[Sphinx](/poses/sphinx)** takes the spine the other way for three minutes — elbows forward if the lower back objects — released in **[Crocodile](/poses/crocodile)**.

**[Reclined Twist](/poses/reclined-twist)** is the long one: three full minutes each side, knees higher for more and lower for less.

Then the part that makes this class what it is. Twelve minutes of guided relaxation in **[Corpse](/poses/corpse)** — a body scan that makes each part heavy in turn, lifting and dropping the feet and hands and head, then a slow repeated cue, and Om Shanti three times to close.

Eighty per cent throughout, and Katie gives the reason: a hundred per cent, held for four minutes, over time is how people hurt themselves.
