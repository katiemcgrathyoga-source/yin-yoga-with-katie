A whole-body practice in twenty minutes, which mostly means it doesn't waste any of them.

**[Puppy](/poses/puppy)** opens the chest and the upper back with the hips staying over the knees, so the stretch goes into the spine rather than the lower back. Two minutes. Then **[Child's Pose](/poses/childs-pose)** takes the spine the other way for two more, which is why it comes straight afterwards.

The middle of the class is one shape doing two jobs: **[Shoelace](/poses/shoelace)** for the outer hips, with **[Cow Face](/poses/cow-face) arms** threaded through it for the shoulders and chest. Both sides, and if your hands don't meet behind your back, they don't need to.

**[Caterpillar](/poses/caterpillar)** is the long one — three minutes down the whole back body, hamstrings and spine together — and a brief **[seated twist](/poses/seated-twist)** on each side closes it before a few quiet breaths.

Holds are two to three minutes, which is where Yin starts to reach past muscle. Nothing here needs a prop, though a blanket under the knees in Puppy is a kindness if they're sensitive.
