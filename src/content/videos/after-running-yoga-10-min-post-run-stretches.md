Ten minutes, straight after your run, while everything is still warm. Katie opens by naming the exact moment this is for: *"if you've just been out running, I'm sure you want to jump in the shower or eat something — so first, let's do this."*

That's the whole design. Short enough that it doesn't compete with the shower.

## What you'll do

Downward Dog to start, paddling the feet and stretching through the calves. Then the same sequence on each side: Three-Legged Dog, into **Lizard** with the front foot on a forty-five-degree angle, then heel-toeing across into **Pigeon** with the front foot flexed to protect the knee. From Pigeon you sit over onto the hip and swing the back leg through for **Half-Shoelace**, which brings the hamstring and calf into it alongside the hip. Windshield Wipers to finish and unwind.

Two cues worth keeping from the class: keep the front foot flexed in Pigeon, and imagine magnets pulling your feet toward each other so the hips stay square rather than collapsing to one side.

## Where this sits

This is a **yang** cool-down — shapes held for a handful of breaths while your muscles are still warm and elastic. It's not Yin, and it isn't trying to be.
