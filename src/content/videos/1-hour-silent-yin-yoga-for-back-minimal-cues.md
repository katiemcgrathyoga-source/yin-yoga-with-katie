A silent hour for the back. Katie names the pose, tells you how long, and then leaves you alone with it.

**[Butterfly](/poses/butterfly)** opens for five minutes, then **[Half Butterfly](/poses/half-butterfly)** for four a side with a minute of windshield wipers between — the wipers are there deliberately, not as filler.

**[Caterpillar](/poses/caterpillar)** for five minutes down the whole back body, countered with Hammock — hands behind you, hips lifted, head dropping back.

**[Sphinx](/poses/sphinx)** is the centre of it, and it's done twice: five minutes, then a minute face down with the head turned one way and that knee drawn up, then five minutes again and the same rest looking the other way. Ten minutes of backbend, broken where it needs breaking.

**[Reclined Twist](/poses/reclined-twist)** for a full five minutes each side.

Then **[Corpse](/poses/corpse)** for ten. A singing bowl sounds near the end so you don't have to wonder.

Six shapes in an hour. If you've found guided classes chatty, this is the other thing.
