steps:
  - pose: "dangling"
    seconds: 180
    note: "Feet hip-width, knees soft, fold and hang. Calves, hamstrings and the lower back, all at once."
  - pose: "dragon"
    seconds: 150
    sides: 2
    note: "Low lunge, back knee down. Sink into the front of the back hip. Keep it in the hip, not the knee."
  - pose: "swan"
    seconds: 120
    sides: 2
    note: "Front shin across, stay upright on your hands. The glute and outer hip of the front leg."
  - pose: "legs-up-the-wall"
    seconds: 300
    note: "Hips close to the wall, legs up, arms soft. Five minutes. Let the legs drain."
faq:
  - q: "How soon after a run should I do this?"
    a: "Within an hour or so, while the tissue is still warm. Straight off the road is ideal. If you'd rather shower first, that's fine too, just don't leave it until you've stiffened up on the sofa."
  - q: "How is this different from the Tight Hips routine?"
    a: "Tight Hips goes deep on one thing, the hips. This one covers the whole chain in four shapes: the back line, the front of the hip, the glutes, then the legs up the wall. It's the one to reach for after a normal run when nothing in particular is complaining."
  - q: "Can I do it in running shoes?"
    a: "Take the shoes off. Everything else you ran in is fine, and you don't need a mat. A towel under the back knee for Dragon helps on a hard floor."
  - q: "What if Swan hurts my knee?"
    a: "Bring the front shin closer to your body so the foot sits near the opposite hip, or lie on your back and do the figure-four version instead. The feeling belongs in the hip, never the knee."
